How to use the charts

  • YOU: say the complete quoted call, replacing bracketed values with the current mission/controller values. Do not say the brackets. Repeat the receiver and your callsign on every transmission.
  • WAIT / FLY: a flight action or telemetry condition, not a radio call. Saying “airborne” or “trapped” cannot substitute for the aircraft actually doing it.
  • Read back the actual clearance, not the sample values. Say the explicit word readback. Runway sides, altimeter and carrier marshal numbers must match exactly.
  • Use only the role/frequency published for the selected field or carrier. Use a unique live human callsign or modex, matching your SRS radio name. Native controllers do not use LLM interpretation.
  • These large charts are intentionally not split into happy-path and exception diagrams. Scroll inside a chart, use its zoom controls, or download the complete SVG. The linked Markdown provides phrase tables, examples and context requirements.
  • Green solid: normal progression
  • Red: refusal or error
  • Amber dashed: retry, recovery or cancellation
  • Gray: optional status or shared checks
